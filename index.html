Renewable vs Non-renewable

Can you explain why an energy source belongs in a particular category?

♻️ Renewable Energy

Renewable energy comes from sources that are naturally replenished over relatively short periods.


  • Sunlight
  • Wind
  • Moving water
  • Biomass

⛏️ Non-renewable Energy

Non-renewable energy comes from resources that are limited and take extremely long periods to form.


  • Coal
  • Natural gas
  • Nuclear fuels such as uranium

🎯 Learning Objectives

By the end of the activity, learners should be able to:

🔹 Identify different energy sources.
🔹 Describe how different energy sources can be used.
🔹 Classify energy sources as renewable or non-renewable.
🔹 Explain the reasoning behind their classifications.
🔹 Communicate and discuss scientific ideas with others.
🔹 Connect classroom learning with energy use in everyday life.

Njabulo Zondo

🎓 Educator in Training

Njabulo Zondo is a Bachelor of Education student and an educator in training with a passion for creating meaningful, learner-centred learning experiences.


The idea behind Energy Explorer comes from a simple belief: learners should not only read about scientific concepts — they should have opportunities to explore, interact, classify, discuss and explain them.


This Grade 7 Natural Sciences learning resource was designed to transform the topic of energy sources into an experience that encourages active participation. The physical energy cards and interactive activities help learners recognise different energy sources and distinguish between renewable and non-renewable energy.

“Learning becomes meaningful when learners are given the opportunity to experience what they are learning.”
